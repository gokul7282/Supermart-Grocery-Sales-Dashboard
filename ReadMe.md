Supermart Grocery Sales Dashboard

📊 Project Overview

The Supermart Grocery Sales Dashboard is an Excel-based business analytics project developed to analyze grocery sales performance.

The dashboard helps management understand overall sales, profit, product performance, discounts, and geographic performance through an interactive and easy-to-understand business dashboard.


🎯 Project Objectives

1. Understand Overall Sales Performance

- What are the total sales and total profit?
- Which product categories contribute the largest share of total sales?
- How do sales change over time?
- Which regions generate the highest sales?

2. Understand Product Performance

- Which categories and sub-categories generate the highest sales?
- Which products or sub-categories have high sales but comparatively low profit?

3. Understand Profit and Discount

- Which categories and regions generate the highest profit?
- Is there a relationship between discount and profit?

4. Understand Geographic Performance

- Which states and cities generate the highest sales?
- Which locations have high sales but comparatively low profit?

📈 Dashboard Analysis

The dashboard focuses on four major areas:

Overall Sales Performance
Analyzes total sales, total profit, category contribution, sales trends over time, and regional performance.

Product Performance
Compares category and sub-category sales and identifies areas where sales are high but profit is comparatively low.

Profit and Discount
Analyzes profit performance across categories and regions and examines the relationship between discount and profit.

Geographic Performance
Analyzes sales performance across states and cities and identifies locations with high sales but comparatively low profit.



💡 Key Business Questions

The dashboard was designed to help management quickly answer important business questions without reviewing the complete sales dataset.

It provides a consolidated view of:

- Sales performance
- Profit performance
- Product performance
- Discount impact
- Regional performance
- State and city performance
